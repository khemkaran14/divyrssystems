# Outreach

## Who to contact (build a list of 100)
- **NBFCs & HFCs:** Chief Compliance Officer, Head of Compliance, Company Secretary
- **Fintechs / payment aggregators / lending apps:** Head of Compliance, CTO
- **Brokers, AMCs, PMS, RIAs:** Compliance Officer
- **Software vendors (GRC, loan-origination, KYB/onboarding):** CTO, Head of Product. One deal can serve hundreds of their customers.
- **Law firms & CA/CS firms with BFSI practices:** partners

Where to find them: LinkedIn search by title + company type, RBI's list of registered NBFCs, SEBI's intermediary lists, fintech association member lists.

> Hi {first name}, I run Divyrs RegData: every RBI and SEBI circular turned into obligations and deadlines within minutes, plus a penalty search for vendors. Building it with compliance teams at NBFCs. Would value 15 minutes of your view on it.
