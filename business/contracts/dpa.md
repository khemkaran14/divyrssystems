# Data processing agreement (template, have a lawyer review)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, between **{Customer}** (Data Fiduciary) and **Divyrs Systems** (Data Processor).

1. **Scope.** Divyrs processes personal data only to provide RegData and related services: account users' names, emails and phone numbers; and names Customer submits for enforcement screening or watchlists.
2. **Instructions.** Divyrs processes the data only on Customer's documented instructions (using the service as designed) and not for its own purposes.
3. **Security.** Encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, access limited to authorised personnel, daily backups, and the other measures at divyrs.com/security.
4. **Sub-processors.** Hosting ({Hostinger, India/EU}), email delivery ({Hostinger mail}), payments (Razorpay), AI processing of *public regulator documents only* (Anthropic). Divyrs will tell Customer before adding a sub-processor that receives Customer personal data.
5. **Breaches.** Divyrs tells Customer without undue delay, and within {24} hours of becoming aware, of a personal data breach affecting Customer data, with the information Customer needs to notify the Data Protection Board and affected people.
6. **Data principals' requests.** Divyrs helps Customer respond to access, correction and erasure requests.
7. **Retention and deletion.** On termination, Divyrs deletes Customer personal data within {30} days, except what law requires it to keep (e.g. invoices).
8. **Audit.** Divyrs answers reasonable security questionnaires once a year and on any breach.
9. **Transfers.** Data may be processed outside India only as permitted under the Act.
