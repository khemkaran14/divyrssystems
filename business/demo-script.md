# 20-minute RegData demo

**Before the call:** know their entity type (NBFC, broker, PA…). Create a free key for them and set up a digest filtered to their type so they get a real email the next morning.

1. **Their world (3 min).** "How does your team track new RBI/SEBI circulars today? Who reads them? How do you prove to auditors you acted on each one? Have you ever missed one?" Write down their words; use them later.
2. **The feed (4 min).** Open divyrs.com/regdata/topics/<their topic>. Click a recent high-impact item: summary, obligations with deadlines and PDF page, "applies to", amended circulars, the official source link.
3. **Enforcement (4 min).** On /regdata/enforcement, search one of *their* vendors or competitors. Then show bulk screening: "send us your vendor list, we flag anyone penalised."
4. **Zero-effort alerts (3 min).** Dashboard → email digest with their filters plus a watchlist. Click "Send preview" so it lands in their inbox during the call.
5. **For their tech team (3 min).** /regdata/docs: one curl call, the OpenAPI file, a webhook test.
6. **Close (3 min).** "Shall we run a 60-day pilot with your team? If it saves your analysts time and you don't miss anything, we move to an annual plan." Agree a start date and who will use it.

**After:** send the one-pager plus the pilot agreement the same day. Diary a check-in for week 2 and week 6.
