# Divyrs RegData
**India's regulatory data, as an API** · divyrs.com/regdata

## The problem
Compliance teams at banks, NBFCs, fintechs and brokers read RBI and SEBI websites by hand every day, copy circulars into spreadsheets and hope nothing is missed. A missed direction means a deadline missed, an audit finding or a penalty. Lenders and procurement teams check partners against regulator penalties one search at a time.

## What RegData does
- **Regulatory Change API**: every new RBI and SEBI circular, direction and notification within minutes, with a plain-English summary, **obligations, deadlines, effective dates, the entity types it applies to and the earlier rules it amends**, each linked to the page of the official PDF.
- **Enforcement Actions API**: penalties, adjudication and settlement orders, licence cancellations and restrictions, searchable by company or person with fuzzy name matching. **Screen up to 100 vendors or borrowers in one call.**
- **Alerts without coding**: daily email digests filtered to your entity type, watchlists for names you care about, Excel export, and webhooks for your systems.

## Why teams choose it
- **Official sources only**, every record cited. High-impact items are verified by a person.
- **Fast**: feeds checked every 30 minutes.
